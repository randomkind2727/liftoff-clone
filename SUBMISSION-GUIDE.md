## ✅ WHAT'S DONE

1. **Full mobile-first PWA app built** — TypeScript, Tailwind, Zustand, Framer Motion, Recharts
2. **5 tabs with rich features:**
   - 🏠 **Home** — Level card, XP progress, streak/coins stats, daily summary, active challenges, achievements preview
   - 🏋️ **Workout** — Real-time timer, 10 workout types, 4 intensity levels, live calorie/XP counter, completion animation with confetti
   - 👥 **Social** — Leaderboard with friends (medals for top 3), your rank, weekly challenge card
   - 📊 **Progress** — 14-day XP area chart, workout frequency bar chart, workout type pie chart, summary stats
   - 👤 **Profile** — All-time stats, 20 achievements (4 rarity tiers), body metrics tracking, settings
3. **Beautiful dark UI** — Glass morphism, animated gradient background, ambient orbs, shimmer effects, glow shadows
4. **Innovative features:**
   - XP system with intensity multipliers (beast mode = 2x XP)
   - Coin economy (earn from workouts, spend on rewards)
   - 20 achievements (common/rare/epic/legendary)
   - Weekly challenges with coin rewards
   - Social leaderboard competition
   - Body metrics tracking (weight, body fat)
   - Confetti celebration on workout completion
   - Demo data for immediate testing
5. **Zero build errors** — compiles cleanly with Next.js 16 Turbopack
6. **Deployed to Vercel** — live URL below
